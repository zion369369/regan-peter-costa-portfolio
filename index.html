Clinical Competencies & Technical Capabilities

Hemodialysis Machinery

Independent priming, calibration, programming, and troubleshooting of major international dialysis systems:

  • Fresenius Medical Care (4008S, 5008 CorDiax)
  • B. Braun Dialog+ Hemodialysis Machines
  • Gambro AK 96 / Artis Physio
  • Bedside SLED Systems for ICU/HDU renal failure

Vascular Access Cannulation

Specialized high-precision access maintenance preserving patient vascular lifespan:

  • Native Arteriovenous Fistula (AVF) Cannulation
  • Arteriovenous Graft (AVG) puncture & surveillance
  • Rope-ladder & Buttonhole cannulation techniques
  • Fistula physical assessment (Bruit, Thrill, Steal syndrome)

CVC & Permcath Management

Rigorous catheter maintenance adhering strictly to Aseptic Non-Touch Technique (ANTT):

  • Tunneled Permcath and non-tunneled temporary CVCs
  • Heparin locking (1:1000, 1:5000) & 4% Citrate locks
  • Catheter thrombosis clot clearance protocols
  • Zero CRBSI track record under direct shift management

Emergency & Complications

Rapid protocolized resuscitation for acute intradialytic physiological decompensation:

  • Acute Hypotension (UF zeroing, Trendelenburg, 0.9% Saline)
  • Dialysis Disequilibrium Syndrome (DDS) intervention
  • Dialyzer first-use hypersensitivity & acute hemolysis
  • Air Embolism emergency response & BLS resuscitation

Water Plant & RO Purity

Monitoring dialysis water safety according to strict AAMI / ISO 23500 international directives:

  • Daily pre/post carbon total chlorine & chloramine (<0.1 ppm)
  • TDS, conductivity, and water hardness logging
  • Chemical & thermal disinfection of RO distribution loop
  • Bacterial & Endotoxin safety compliance

Pharmacotherapy & Adequacy

Dialysis adequacy tracking and safe administration of complex renal therapeutics:

  • Dialysis Adequacy: Urea kinetic modeling (Kt/V ≥ 1.3, URR > 68%)
  • Intradialytic blood & packed red blood cell transfusion
  • Erythropoietin (EPO), Darbepoetin, IV Iron sucrose
  • Heparinized systemic protocols vs. Heparin-free saline flushes
