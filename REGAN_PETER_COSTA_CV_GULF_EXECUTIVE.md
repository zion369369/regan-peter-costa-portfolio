## SPECIALIZED CLINICAL COMPETENCIES
* **Dialysis Systems & Technologies:** Independent priming, operation, and maintenance of Fresenius 4008S, 5008 CorDiax, B. Braun Dialog+, Gambro AK 96, and ICU-based SLED therapies.
* **Vascular Access Cannulation:** High-expertise cannulation of native Arteriovenous Fistulas (AVF) and Grafts (AVG); fistula assessment (bruit, thrill, steal syndrome surveillance); buttonhole and rope-ladder methods.
* **Central Venous Catheter Care:** Permcath and temporary dual-lumen catheter access, heparin lock instillation (1000 IU/mL, 5000 IU/mL), citrate locks, strict Aseptic Non-Touch Technique (ANTT) dressing protocols.
* **Acute Complications Management:** Protocolized response for intradialytic hypotension, disequilibrium syndrome, dialyzer hypersensitivity, air embolism, cardiac arrhythmias, and acute vascular access clotting.
* **Dialysis Adequacy & Laboratory Surveillance:** Calculation of Dry Weight, Ultrafiltration (UF) rates, Pre/Post-BUN sampling, $Kt/V$ ratio calculation ($\ge 1.3$), Urea Reduction Ratio ($URR > 68\%$).
* **Water Treatment Plant Protocol:** Daily total chlorine/chloramine monitoring (< 0.1 ppm), TDS, hardness, and RO water purification adherence conforming to AAMI / ISO 23500 hemodialysis standards.
* **Infection Prevention & Control:** Strict isolation bay protocols for Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, and HIV seropositive patients; dialyzer handling and machine chemical/thermal disinfection.
