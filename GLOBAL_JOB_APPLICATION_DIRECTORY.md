## 6. REGAN'S 3-TRACK STRATEGIC ROADMAP

### Track 1: The Fastest Departure (Middle East - 2 to 4 Months)
1. Initiate **DataFlow Primary Source Verification (PSV)** for his Diploma, Square Hospital certificate, and BNMC license.
2. Book and sit the **Saudi Prometric** or **DHA Prometric** exam at the Dhaka Prometric test center.
3. Apply directly to **Diaverum KSA**, **DaVita KSA**, or **SEHA UAE**. With 10+ years at Square Hospital, he will be fast-tracked to Senior Dialysis Staff Nurse.

### Track 2: The Western English-Speaking Route (UK & Ireland - 4 to 8 Months)
1. Prepare and sit the **OET (Occupational English Test - Nursing)** aiming for Grade B (or IELTS Academic 7.0/6.5).
2. Register with the **UK NMC**, receive the Authorization to Test, and sit the **NMC CBT (Adult Nursing)** in Dhaka.
3. Apply directly on **NHS Jobs** ([jobs.nhs.uk](https://www.jobs.nhs.uk)) and **Trac Jobs** ([trac.jobs](https://www.trac.jobs)) searching for *"Renal Nurse"* / *"Haemodialysis Nurse"*.
4. Attend remote Zoom/Teams NHS interview, obtain Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS), fly to the UK with visa sponsored by the NHS Trust, complete OSCE, and receive full UK NMC Band 5/6 PIN.

### Track 3: The Long-Term US Green Card Route (12 to 18 Months)
1. Enroll in an NCLEX-RN prep course and register with an international-friendly US State Board of Nursing (e.g., Montana, New York).
2. Sit the **NCLEX-RN** at an international Pearson VUE center.
3. Sign directly with **DaVita US** or **Avant/Conexus** for an **EB-3 immigrant visa (Permanent Residency / Green Card)**.
